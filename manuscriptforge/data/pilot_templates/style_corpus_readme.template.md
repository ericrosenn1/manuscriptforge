## Suggested Files

- Manuscript sections written by the target author.
- Methods, Results, Discussion, limitations, response-to-reviewers, or cover-letter text.
- Files with clear headings and minimal unrelated correspondence.

## Privacy Review

- Remove identifiers or sensitive collaborator details when possible.
- Keep samples local unless you explicitly approve another workflow.
- Do not include files from authors whose style should not be imitated.
