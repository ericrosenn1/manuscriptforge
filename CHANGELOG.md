# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-31

- Added resumable research workflows, isolated workers, verified caching and export bundles.
- Serialized cancellation, worker admission, result acceptance, and finalization to prevent cancellation races; added deterministic regression coverage.
- Added budgeted provider calls, quote-anchored fact proposals, and quantitative/citation guards.
- Added source-bound author claim decisions, stale-review detection and explicit section routing; models cannot approve claims.
- Limited manuscript bibliographies to cited records while retaining the full source citation registry.
- Added indexed lexical retrieval and a trained, explicitly labeled style-preference ranker.
- Added grouped evaluation protocols, versioned quantity scoring, and optional raw-response diagnostics.
- Added the corrected quantity v1.1 input contract and a locked development/test comparison with its pre-test selection decision preserved.
- Updated the synthetic research demo with clearly labeled scripted fixture approvals.
- Preserved the original corpus CLI and separate browser Studio.
- Corrected empty-evidence style scoring, weak citation matching, numeric context checks, and selected regression failures.

Measured results and limitations are recorded in the [0.2.0 release notes](docs/release-0.2.0.md). Development-set results, synthetic mechanism checks, and local performance timings do not establish scientific or real-author validity.
